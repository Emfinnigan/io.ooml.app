Frequently Asked Questions

What macOS version do I need?

OOML requires macOS 12 (Monterey) or later. It's optimized for Apple Silicon Macs but also works on Intel Macs.

Do I need an internet connection?

No! OOML works completely offline. You only need internet if you choose to use the optional share link feature.

Where are my recordings saved?

All recordings are saved locally on your Mac. You can find them in the default location you set, or access them directly from within the app.
